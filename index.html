Liam Peet-Paré

I am a business and technology lawyer at McCarthy Tétrault in Vancouver. I work in M&A, corporate governance, and securities and capital markets, alongside tech work involving AI governance, IP work, and some commercial contracting. I have a dual role with the firm's AI innovation team where I help the firm to transition into the AI era by assisting the roll out of AI tools, developing educational materials, designing AI workflows, and building AI-powered tools. I was called to the British Columbia bar in 2026.

Before law I completed an MSc in statistical machine learning at the University of Alberta, with Amii, under the supervision of Alona Fyshe and Nidhi Hegde. Prior to graduate school, I worked as a data scientist and researcher for several years, and completed undergraduate degrees in mathematics and political science and economics.

I think the intersection of law, politics, economics, and AI is the most fascinating and important space in the world currently. We are rapidly approaching civilization-upending change and are woefully unprepared for it. I believe that to understand the moment we are in requires both a deep understanding of AI and the foundations and structures upon which our society is built.

This is a personal website and is in no way affiliated with McCarthy Tétrault or any of its clients. The views expressed on this website are entirely my own and do not represent the views of McCarthy Tétrault.

Prototypes

Working prototypes built to test whether a piece of legal work could be automated. I think the legal profession has barely scratched the surface in terms of reimagining law in the world of AI.

What's market?

Reads a corpus of public M&A agreements into structured deal terms, then benchmarks, compares and searches them.

M&A Market analytics Precedent search

Due diligence automation

Turns a folder of agreements into a structured diligence table. Similar to offerings within Harvey, Legora, and CoCounsel.

M&A Document review LLM extraction

Deal spaces

A shared closing agenda and document room for the firms on a deal, with version history and redlines.

M&A Closing Collaboration

Regulatory Form Automation

Automates aspects of responses to complex regulatory asks in the context of M&A deals. Easily generalizable to arbitrary regulatory responses.
